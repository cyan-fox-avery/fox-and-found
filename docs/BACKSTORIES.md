# Fox & Found — Backstory Sketches

Raw material, not canon. Avery's approved keepers as of 2026-10-10.
**Placement in the campaign is undecided** — that decision comes later.
Each sketch is a starting point for a full case write-up per `docs/CASE-TEMPLATE.md`;
every finished case still needs its one primary design purpose.

---

## The platinum pet

Bought at eight weeks old from a breeder's website promising "dog-like,
litter-trainable." Lived in a second-floor apartment. Foxes musk — everything
the owner owned smelled like fox within a month. He chewed through a couch, a
doorframe, and the landlord's patience.

The surrender form, in the owner's handwriting: *"He's a good boy. He just
can't be what I wanted him to be."*

- Morph: platinum (see `docs/MORPH-PLAN.md`).
- Design purpose (set): unreleasable — human-habituated, never learned to live
  as a wild fox. Wellbeing/enrichment milestones, not release-directed.
  Mirror image of FOX-002's arc.

## The amber producer

From the private breeder shutdown. A young vixen, the breeder's best
producer — three litters, valued by the litter. Kept in a wire hutch; never
touched grass. When the seizure team opened the hutch she didn't run — she
froze.

- Morph: amber.
- Design purpose: open (trust-building? first grass?).

## The silver retiree

Same breeder, older. Past breeding age — the ledger had a date next to her
name, and the seizure came a week before it.

- Morph: silver.
- Design purpose: open. Note: she is old but living — do not blur with the
  authored elderly-death beat at the sister sanctuary.

## The farmhand's call

The fur farm didn't close because of a raid. The owner died, the son wanted
out, and the farmhand who'd fed the foxes for six years made the call because
nobody else would. He asked if he could visit sometime.

- Morphs: mixed — mostly silvers, plus others (see `docs/MORPH-PLAN.md`).
- Design purpose: open (scale/triage? the biggest single intake event?).

## The white pair

Two arctic foxes from the same farm, a bonded pair. They've only ever had
each other. Harley takes one look and starts dialing north.

- Species: arctic fox — the exception that proves the red-fox-rescue rule.
- Design purpose (set): the "not us, ever" referral — not every fox is a red
  fox, and recognizing a different species' needs *is* the welfare decision.
  Success condition is the referral itself. See `docs/MORPH-PLAN.md`.
