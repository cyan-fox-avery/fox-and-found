## FOX-002 — First rehabilitation case

- **Species:** Red fox (*Vulpes vulpes*)
- **Sex:** Female
- **Age:** Young adult
- **Intake reason:** Sideswiped by a vehicle; suspected hind-leg fracture
- **Assessment:** Significant soft-tissue injury/bruising; no fracture
- **Prognosis:** Excellent
- **Release potential:** High
- **Temperament:** Extremely wary and defensive around humans
- **Naming:** Player first; Harley may choose if skipped
- **Expected duration:** About 5–7 weeks, subject to tuning

### Core milestones
1. Intake and fracture concern ruled out
2. Camera footage shows increasing weight-bearing
3. Reassessment and move to rehab run
4. Outdoor conditioning
5. Strong human avoidance and normal wild behaviour confirmed
6. Release assessment
7. Release

### Design purpose
Core rehabilitation loop, camera observation, naming, milestone progress, and the lesson that human avoidance is a good sign in a releasable fox.

## FOX-003 — Demodectic mange case

- **Species:** Red fox (*Vulpes vulpes*)
- **Sex:** Male
- **Age:** Older adult
- **Appearance:** Very dark auburn/red coat; permanently wonky/notched ear
- **Intake reason:** Patchy hair loss and irritated skin; suspected mange
- **Diagnosis:** Localized demodectic mange / demodicosis
- **General condition:** Somewhat underweight and run-down but stable
- **Prognosis:** Good
- **Release potential:** Good

### Design purpose
Diagnosis before assumption. Coat regrowth and improved body condition provide visible recovery milestones.

## FOX-004 — Sarcoptic mange referral

- **Species:** Red fox (*Vulpes vulpes*)
- **Sex:** Female
- **Age:** Subadult
- **Intake concern:** Extensive hair loss, crusting skin, persistent scratching
- **Diagnosis direction:** Sarcoptic mange
- **Early-game disposition:** Refer to qualified partner facility
- **Reason:** Fox & Found lacks infectious-disease isolation
- **Outcome direction:** Partner treatment → outdoor rehabilitation → release

### Design purpose
First major responsible-referral case and the motivation for Infectious-Disease Isolation.

## FOX-005 — Entanglement / capacity case

- **Species:** Red fox (*Vulpes vulpes*)
- **Sex:** Male
- **Age:** Subadult
- **Intake reason:** Entangled in discarded garden/netting material
- **Condition:** Superficial abrasions, mild dehydration, high stress; no major injury
- **Prognosis:** Excellent
- **Release potential:** High
- **Expected duration:** About 2–4 weeks, subject to tuning

### Design purpose
Teaches that an empty intake room is not the same as having capacity for an entire case. Also introduces wildlife entanglement hazards.
