# Fox & Found — Naming Achievements

Proposed by Harley (HarlsTheBard) on Discord, 2026-10-21. Avery's response:
"that's going directly in the Pull Request." Status: proposal, not canon —
the game brief does not currently include an achievements system at all
(see open questions).

## The two achievements

### Mikayla

- **Trigger:** the player names a fox **Mikayla**.
- **Why:** in honor of Mikayla from Save-a-Fox (SaveAFox Rescue).
- **Text:** open — Harley proposed the achievement, not the wording.

### Finn / Finnigan

- **Trigger:** the player names a fox **Finn** or **Finnigan**.
- **Text (Harley's wording):** "The fox, the myth, the legend!"
- **Why:** Finn is the Save-a-Fox fox — the name carries the homage.

## Precedent

Tag Along's naming easter eggs work the same way: naming a shark Sarah,
Bruce, Mary Lee, or Nicole triggers a character response (and, for Bruce,
a hidden achievement at the end of a slow chain). Fox & Found's equivalent
of Sarah's threads would be Harley — whether these achievements also come
with a Harley message is an open question, not a decision.

Like Tag Along's Mary Lee and Nicole (real sharks), these honor the real
fox-rescue world rather than inventing references.

## Welfare check

Naming achievements are pure homage — they reward a name, never a care
decision. No conflict with the welfare razor and no conflict with the
no-death rule. A fox named Mikayla or Finn is treated exactly like any
other fox.

## Open questions

- Does Fox & Found get an achievements system at all? The brief (PR #3)
  never mentions one — these two may be the seed of it, or they may live
  as naming easter eggs without a system behind them.
- Visible or hidden? Tag Along's Bruce achievement is hidden; Harley
  proposed these openly, which points toward visible.
- Player-chosen names only, or also names Harley picks from `names.json`
  when the player skips? The homage lands better if the player chose it
  deliberately — but that is a decision, not a given.
- Matching: case-insensitive, Tag Along style (any caps trigger it)?
  Both spellings (Finn and Finnigan) confirmed as triggers?
- Achievement text for Mikayla — Harley's proposal did not include wording.
- Do these come with a Harley message, or is the achievement itself the
  whole beat?
